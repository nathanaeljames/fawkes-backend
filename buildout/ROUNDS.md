# Fawkes Iteration 2 — development rounds

v1, adopted 2026-10-13. Built on Plan v13, Specification v1.16, Contract v6; Fable's 2026-10-12 breakdown with Astra's review items adopted where noted. Round IDs are stable from here on; if a round is later split, the parts become P<n>-<m>a / b, never renumbered.

**Legend.** Mode: D = dual (two implementers), S = solo (one implementer; still a full round: SPEC, tests, BUILD, audit, viva). N = a round where the owner-port step (PIPELINE.md step 9) is expected to be substantial; the owner may also implement blind in a third worktree on any round he chooses. Tests = contract cases (BC-) and plan assertions committed as `xfail(strict=True)` before the round opens. Own = functions the owner explains at the viva. Dep = rounds that must be accepted first. Every round also carries the permanent gates: scope isolation, prior phase exits, source preservation, trace and cost receipts, channel correctness, no raw-audio retention.

**Hardware note.** One RTX 3090 only; the 4B router and the embedder share the card with the 27B (llama.cpp beside vLLM, embedder on CPU until measured). The README's GTX 1660 Super line is withdrawn.
