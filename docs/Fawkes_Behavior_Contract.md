## 7. Uncertainties and conflicts

- **U-1 Resolved (v1.9).** P-14 now names the Layer 1b exception and the status-board projection is defined (title, phase, progress, last step, estimate; no result body). The owner further decided that Layers 1a and 1b load together on cumulative thread identification.
- **U-2 Resolved except one proposal.** Root is web/app-only with its own password, never voice, audited (owner Q3). A second factor for root remains a proposal.
- **U-3 Narrowed.** P-05 settles the silent, never-identified case (record in the thread, no push). The remaining gap is endpoint unavailability inside the 60-second window — a battery client that disconnects before playback — and retry behavior; to be specified with Q-16.
- **U-4 Pass-blurb resistance (Q-15).** The challenge checks are specified; resistance to transformed recordings and synthesized speech is an evaluation target, not a demonstrated result. Voice authentication cannot establish silo entry; voice access to a silo exists only over the WebSocket of an actively signed-in silo session, which bounds the damage.
- **U-5 Deletion and retention (Q-13, GP 2 as qualified).** No deletion may occur without a documented policy; the policy does not exist yet. Behavior BC-27 is therefore "never delete" until an owner decision.
- **U-6 Evidence quality thresholds (C-04, D-12).** "Quality-sufficient" utterances are required for the three-count, but the threshold is a Phase 2 measurement.
- **U-7 Prior decay horizon and follow-up window values.** Both are declared tunables with no numbers; start short, measure.
- **U-8 Resolved (v1.10).** A pass-blurb inside a workspace thread forks it into a user-scoped continuation (BC-47).

## 8. Before Phase 0 versus later

**Needed before Phase 0 (schema and interface freeze):** BC-20 to BC-23, BC-70, BC-72, BC-75, BC-80 (event ids, synchronous search vectors, derived-jobs table, type-specific routing), BC-25 (per-scope hashing), the scope-context contract behind BC-24 and BC-28 (scope kind and id on every content and derived row — identity, device, role, session, and configuration records keep their D-01 authority; permission-keyed cache key shape), nullable `task_id`, the facet enum seed, the silo leakage tests, the D-01 roles and preferred-device columns, and the audio columns of BC-80 (cheap now, expensive later).

**Can wait for their phases:** everything in sections 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6 lands with Phase 1 (validation and confirmation), Phase 2 (wake, sessions, identification, pass-blurb, promotion), Phase 3 (arbiter, research recovery, delivery protocol, cloud budget reservation), and Phase 5 (silo sessions, accounts, app actions). The window values and revocation thresholds are Phase 2 experiments; the root second factor is decided before Phase 5; U-5 before any retention feature; U-4 is a standing measurement.
